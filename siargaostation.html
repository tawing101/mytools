About Siargao Tides
Siargao has a semi-diurnal tide pattern — two high and two low tides per day. Cloud 9 breaks best on mid-to-high incoming tide with offshore (NE) winds. Island hopping is safest when wave height is below 1m.

For boat trips, diving, or fishing, always use the official tide tables above — never rely on an estimate.
